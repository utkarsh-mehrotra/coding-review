Shortest Path Algorithms

The follow-up interviewers ask: "your graph has 10^5 nodes but is sparse — why not Floyd-Warshall?" Johnson's is the answer that shows depth.

AlgorithmConstraintComplexity
DijkstraNon-negative weights onlyO((V+E) log V)
Bellman-FordHandles negative weights; detects negative cyclesO(V·E)
Floyd-WarshallAll-Pairs Shortest Path, dense graphsO(V³)
Johnson'sAPSP on sparse graphs, beats Floyd-Warshall when E << V²O(V² log V + VE)
A* SearchNeeds admissible heuristic; pathfinding/spatial/games~Dijkstra, faster in practice
