Dijkstra & Shortest-Path Variants

Interviewers rarely ask for plain Dijkstra. They add a constraint, change what a path costs, or ask for more than a distance. Each row is one small change to the base template in section 02.

At most K stops, fuel, keys, obstacle budgetThe state becomes (node, used). Dijkstra if costs differ, plain BFS if every step costs 1.

Cost is the worst edge on the pathCombine with max() instead of +. Same heap loop.
