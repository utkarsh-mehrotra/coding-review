Implicit Graphs: Grids & State Spaces

Most FAANG graph questions never hand you a graph. The nodes are grid cells, board positions or strings, and the edges are the legal moves. The skill is naming the node and generating neighbors lazily; the traversal itself is plain BFS or DFS.

Problem shapeNode isTechnique
