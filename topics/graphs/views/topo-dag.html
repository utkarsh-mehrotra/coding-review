Topological Sort & DAG Patterns

Works only on Directed Acyclic Graphs. Once topologically sorted, many problems reduce to simple DP over the order — longest/shortest path, counting paths, critical path scheduling.

TechniqueIdea
Kahn's (BFS)Queue of zero-in-degree nodes; fewer than V processed at the end → cycle exists
DFS-based topo sortPost-order push onto a stack; reverse at the end
DAG DPRelax edges in topo order — no need for Dijkstra since a DAG has no cycles
